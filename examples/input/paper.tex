\title{Sparse Routing & Long Contexts}
\author{L. Chen}

\begin{abstract}
We study sparse routing & its effect on long contexts, with a budget of 32k tokens.
\end{abstract}

\section{Method}
The router keeps a fixed budget per token. See Figure~\ref{fig:arch} for the layout.
Our implementation follows \cite{vasquez2022}.

We reach 92.1% exact match on the standard split, within 0.3 points of prior work.
